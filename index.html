It all starts with curiosity. I kept asking how a drawing becomes something people can actually use.

I started on paper, in Zambales. A sketch, a pencil, a problem to solve — not decoration.

Then the desk got glossy. Bright, loud, impressive — and hard to use. Beauty for beauty's sake doesn't sell.

I don't decorate. I solve the task: so people notice it, and so it works.

One launch broke. Sketch not found. Blank page. The deadline didn't move.

I fixed it line by line. No magic, no excuses — test, fix, test again.

The calm version shipped. Not the loudest work — the one that worked. People used it, and it sold.

Still curious. Still solving. If you have a task, not just a pretty idea — let's build it.

01 / Profile

Hi, I'm Rj.

Full Stack Developer & Web Designer · Zambales

Zambales, PH · UTC+8

I'm a Full Stack Developer and Web Designer from Zambales. I turn messy, real-world tasks into clean websites and working web apps — from the first sketch on paper to the database behind the screen.

Most projects don't fail because the colors were wrong — they fail because the workflow was confusing, the queries were slow, or the deadline met an untested launch. I care about how a page looks, and I care even more about whether people can finish what they came to do.

From campus attendance and clearance systems to hotel management modules and digital flipbooks, I build end-to-end with HTML/CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, and Supabase.
